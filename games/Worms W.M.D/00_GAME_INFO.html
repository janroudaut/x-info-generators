Worms W.M.D

Description

Join the fight and command your worm warriors into battle in the iconic 2D arcade artillery shooter where serious strategy and silly weaponry decide who swims or sinks.

Wage Widespread Worm Warfare

Unleash tactical, turn-based total chaos in gloriously hand-drawn artillery battlegrounds. Arm your invertebrate platoons with a massive arsenal, command heavy machinery, and use the landscape to your advantage. Outsmart your rivals, blow enormous holes in their plans — and preferably the ground beneath them — to blast them off the map and be the last worms standing.

Artillery Combat on a Whole New Scale

Ready your Holy Hand Grenade, round up the Super Sheep and prepare to drop the Concrete Donkey. Blast your way through 30 campaign levels with over 80 weapons & utilities at your fingertips. Take the high ground, tunnel to safety, set the perfect trap — or forget the plan entirely and reach for the explosives.

The More the Squirmier

Worms W.M.D comes locked, loaded and stuffed with ways to wage war. Hone your skills in Training Missions, take on the Campaign and tackle Challenges solo. Train up then head online to wreak havoc in multiplayer modes and take on up to five opponents in hilarious all-out tactical combat in local, online or ranked play.

Form alliances. Break alliances. Accidentally bazooka yourself into the sea. Whatever happens, make sure your worms are the last ones standing.
